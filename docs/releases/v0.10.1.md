# Harness Slides 0.10.1

Fixes found by building a 19-slide deck with compositions as a user would.

- **Cover, section and closing slides.** A slide can name any layout in the brand
  template and fill its title and subtitle placeholders. `compose layouts` lists
  them. Earlier, every slide used one content layout.
- **Every fit problem in one pass.** `compose compile` reports each slide that
  needs a change, not only the first.
- **Card alignment.** A box with children can place its content at the top,
  middle or bottom.
- **Labels on slanted arrows** are placed beside the line, clear of every node.
  Earlier they sat on the line.
- **Contrast is judged against the card.** Text on a filled card was flagged as
  low contrast against the slide background. Fifteen of seventeen findings on the
  test deck were this false alarm.
- **Icons that blend into their card** are flagged.
- Cover, section and closing slides no longer trigger the text-only and
  repeated-layout screens.

Current public guidance requires runtime 0.10.1.

## Verification

Unit and package tests cover each fix. A 25-slide deck with a dark cover, four
section breaks and a closing slide was built against a corporate template,
previewed through Google and inspected by eye. The combined Tart gate was not
run, and the output was not opened in PowerPoint.
