# Harness Slides 0.11.0

Composed decks get one reading path, a compiler that reports everything at
once, and a parallel build for long decks. Two fresh agents built the same two
decks before and after: failed compiles fell from 20 to 6, and both decks were
better. A parallel build of an 18-slide deck took 25 minutes.

## Guidance

- **One path for a new deck:** design, walkthrough, compositions. The other
  methods sit behind one page, for native tables, charts and what compositions
  cannot express.
- **No conflicting rules:** one place for the approval record, one per-slide
  record, one color rule, and a table of which review checks apply to each
  method. The composed-deck reading path is shorter than in 0.10.2.
- **Final review fixes something:** name the three weakest slides, fix them and
  preview again. A slide gets two revisions at most.
- **Live decks stay at the live size:** do not step text down a role to fit more
  in; `preview` reports `reading-size-in-live-deck`.
- **Parallel build:** for more than about ten content slides, a lead splits the
  deck into sections of three or four slides, briefs one worker per section,
  merges and reviews the deck as a whole. Workers design their slides.

## Compiler

- **Everything in one run:** every rule break on every slide, every part that
  does not fit, unknown icons and layouts. Text shortfalls are given in lines,
  and a column lists what each part needs.
- **Check without writing:** `compose compile` with no `--output`.
- **Brand sizes:** `compose contract --brand` prints the content area, title
  lines, gaps, padding and line heights.
- **`compose merge`** joins section files and refuses a part whose direction or
  IDs clash.
- **No limit on fill colors.** The direction's declared meanings are the limit.
- **Text color:** plain ink gives way to the on-color wherever that reads better,
  so a mid-dark fill gets light text. A focal box may set its text color.
- **Icons** take a `color` and a `style` (solid, outline, plain), and keep their
  own width in a row.
- **Shaped cards** keep their content inside the shape's text area.
- **`weight-overridden`** is reported when content forces a different share;
  empty boxes have no minimum, so bars keep their scale.
- **A `parallel` relation** for peers of equal standing. Nesting goes to eight
  levels.

## Changed behavior

- In a row, `align` of `start`, `center` or `end` now gives every child the
  height its content needs, boxes included. The default still fills the row.
- An icon in a row takes its own width unless it has a weight.
- A composition that relied on the three-fill limit to fail no longer fails.

Current public guidance requires runtime 0.11.0.

## Verification

Unit tests cover each change: 170 pass, 3 skipped; two studio tests need local
port binding and fail only inside a sandbox. Four fresh-agent builds of two
8-slide decks and three parallel builds were rendered through Google Slides and
inspected by eye. The combined Tart gate was not run, and no output was opened
in PowerPoint.
